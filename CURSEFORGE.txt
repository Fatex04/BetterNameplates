BetterNameplates - CurseForge
=============================

English summary
---------------
BetterNameplates is a lightweight, taint-safe extension for Blizzard's Modern nameplates in WoW Classic Era that adds compact scaling, in-bar unit levels, and fully customizable dynamic threat percentages without replacing the default nameplates.

Categories
----------
Main category: Unit Frames
Additional categories: Combat, Tank
